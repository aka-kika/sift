## What it is

<!-- SF Symbol: app -->
Sift is a native macOS app that audits everything in /Applications: it explains
what each app is, scores how well it fits your workflow, tracks updates, and
keeps a record of what you own — license keys, subscriptions, and App Store
purchases. Analysis runs on local AI by default, grounded in evidence: the app's
own metadata, its website's words, and what you actually use. Built by
**Veronica Loren** ([aka-kika](https://github.com/aka-kika)) — the app she uses
every day to decide what stays on her Mac.

## The milestone

<!-- SF Symbol: flag.checkered -->
1.3.0 rounds out ownership. Beyond license keys, Sift now tracks **subscriptions**
(price, cycle, next renewal with a live countdown) and **App Store purchases**
(provenance you didn't have to enter, plus an optional Paid mark). The License
Vault grew into a complete **"everything you've bought" ledger**. Settings got
calmer — Apple Intelligence is the zero-setup hero, the other engines tuck under
Advanced — and the analysis Style note became a global, every-engine preference
living with your Profile. Plus a round of dark-mode and detail-panel polish.

## What shipped in 1.3.0

<!-- SF Symbol: shippingbox -->
- **Subscription tracking** — a 💳 row per app: amount + currency, Monthly/Yearly,
  next renewal with a "renews in N days" countdown that turns amber within a week
  and rolls forward automatically. Exported to CSV (price / cycle / renewal).
- **App Store ownership** — App Store apps are auto-detected and show
  "Mac App Store · Tied to your Apple ID" where a license key would be (there is
  none), with an optional **Paid** mark. New CSV `Source` (App Store / Sparkle /
  Homebrew / Other) and `Paid` columns.
- **Paid badge by the app name** — a green seal joins the favorite / my-app /
  subscription / key markers in the list, so an owned app reads as owned at a
  glance.
- **License Vault → purchases ledger** — now lists license-key apps *and* paid
  App Store apps (keyless "Mac App Store · Paid" rows), Installed / No longer
  installed.
- **Settings declutter** — the Models tab leads with the active engine; the engine
  picker and Ollama/cloud configs live under a collapsed "Advanced"; the
  meaningless pinned Model row for Apple Intelligence is gone. Capability-aware
  default already picks Apple Intelligence when the Mac supports it.
- **Style note → Profile, global** — the analysis style note moved from the Models
  tab to Profile and now shapes *every* engine's output, not just Apple
  Intelligence (your old value migrates automatically).
- **Polish** — dark-mode score badges use dark numerals on the light yellow/mint
  fills for legibility; the detail-panel utility rows align and reveal their
  controls on hover; the "View Updates" button gets a cleaner icon.
