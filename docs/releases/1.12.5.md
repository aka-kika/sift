Sift 1.12.5 fixes the October 24 code review and its second check: safer uninstalls, honest update badges, and keys that stay where you put them.

**Uninstall**
- **Data only asks first:** with the app row unticked, Sift asks "The app stays installed. Delete only its data?" Cancel is the default.
- **Apps with a dot in the name:** zoom.us.app and similar can be uninstalled again when macOS needs Finder to move them.
- **Quit & Continue waits:** Sift waits for the app to really quit, and checks again right before anything moves.
- **Launch Agents:** a helper is stopped before its file goes to the Trash.
- **Homebrew:** brew uninstall says the app was removed by Homebrew, not put in the Trash, and Done closes the sheet.
- **Fewer surprises:** short bundle IDs no longer match other apps' files, and Finder is asked only for the app itself.

**Updates**
- **Run Update:** Homebrew updates only the copy it installed, and a failed brew says so instead of "finished".
- **No stuck brew:** a brew command that hangs is stopped after a time limit.
- **Honest badges:** a brew warning no longer makes every app read Up to date, a build-only update is not offered twice, and a Sparkle build like 1.5.2 reads "Update to 1.5.2".
- **Only what fits:** App Store updates that need a newer macOS are skipped.
- **Safe redirects:** update feeds must stay on https and a public host, even after a redirect.

**Keys and privacy**
- **Licence keys:** Replace asks for Touch ID, a revealed key is forgotten when the panel closes, and Copy's Touch ID prompt no longer wipes it.
- **A key you are typing:** a first licence key survives a click outside the panel.
- **API keys:** the field never shows the saved key, saves when you close Settings, and has Remove Key. A key goes over plain http only to this Mac.
- **Clipboard:** quitting clears a licence key you copied.
- **Local links:** a saved link whose name points at your Mac or local network is not fetched.
- **Cloud prompts:** app paths no longer include your Mac account name.

**Analysis and data**
- **Saved changes:** a link, note or docs folder saved during a scan is analyzed once the scan ends.
- **Notes:** the Notes sheet saves on Save only. Escape cancels.
- **Locked before analysis:** the app says why it waits and offers Unlock and Analyze.
- **Cleaner answers:** a model's "Let me know if…" stays out of Best use.
- **Old analysis:** a note says when an analysis was written for an older app version.
- **Failed saves:** if the disk refuses a change, a banner says so.
- **Subscriptions:** monthly apps are listed in the License Vault, and an unset renewal date stays empty.

Your data and license keys carry over untouched.
