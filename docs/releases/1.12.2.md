Sift 1.12.2 is a fix release. It makes license keys safer, stops Homebrew from freezing Sift, and makes two copies of the same app behave.

**License keys**
- Right-clicking another app and choosing Edit License now opens that app's own details. Before, the panel could show the previous app's key, and saving could overwrite or delete the wrong key.
- Removing a key now asks for Touch ID, like revealing or copying one. Only the key goes: the license type and email stay.
- The License Vault says "Mac App Store" only for apps that really came from the Store, and Store apps can be marked Paid again.
- The pointing-hand cursor no longer gets stuck after you close the Vault.

**Two copies of one app** (say, a test build next to the real one)
- Uninstalling one copy no longer removes both from the list.
- Each copy gets its own update check.
- The Uninstall panel leaves the settings the copies share unchecked, and quits only the copy you're removing.

**Everything else**
- Homebrew scans and updates no longer freeze when brew prints a lot.
- The "Find similar apps you have" button is back.
- Switching AI model in Settings shows the "model changed" banner right away.
- Links typed without https (like raycast.com) now open and work for update checks.
- If the AI's answer can't be read, Sift shows an error you can retry instead of saving gibberish as the description.
- Removing an app's link re-checks the app.
- "Using local AI" shows only when the AI really runs on your Mac.
- The CSV export's Paid column now counts any saved key, license type or Paid mark.

Your data and license keys carry over untouched.
