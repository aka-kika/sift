## What it is

<!-- SF Symbol: app -->
Sift is a native macOS app that audits my installed apps with a local model —
what each app is, whether it earns its place in my workflow, what needs updating,
which licenses and subscriptions I pay for. The distinct thing: analysis is
grounded and local-first — evidence, not guesses, and nothing leaves the Mac by
default.

Built by **Kika** ([aka-kika](https://github.com/aka-kika)). The app I open to
decide what stays on my Mac.

## The milestone

<!-- SF Symbol: flag.checkered -->
1.9.0 taught Sift to update itself; 1.10.0 is the first update it gets to
deliver. It is also the release that removes the last excuse not to try Sift:
until now a full audit needed a local Ollama model or a paid key. Gemini and
OpenRouter both have free tiers, so a Mac with nothing installed can run the
whole thing at no cost — which matters for a free beta.

## What shipped in 1.10.0

<!-- SF Symbol: shippingbox -->
- **Google Gemini** — through Gemini's OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Default `gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, the free tier's most generous daily quota; the picker lists the rest.
- **OpenRouter** — one key, hundreds of models. Default `openrouter/free`, the alias for whatever is free that day; `:free` models sort first.
- **One transport for three** — OpenAI, Gemini and OpenRouter now share `OpenAICompatibleService`; the old `OpenAIService` is gone. A 429 says "daily free quota reached" instead of blaming the key.
- **Free during the beta** — the site says so, and every download button reports a `download` event to Vercel Web Analytics so the 14-day number is real.

## The story (how 1.10.0 came together)

<!-- SF Symbol: book -->
Pricing came up and the answer was the calm one: free during the beta, count
downloads for fourteen days, decide with a number instead of a guess. That made
the next question obvious — a beta that needs a paid key or a local model is not
really free. Gemini and OpenRouter both speak OpenAI's dialect, so instead of two
new services the OpenAI one became a table of endpoints. The OpenRouter default
picked from memory turned out not to exist any more; the live model list did,
and so did `openrouter/free`, which is the kind of default that survives.
