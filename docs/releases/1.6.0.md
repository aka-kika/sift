## What it is

<!-- SF Symbol: app -->
Sift is a native macOS app that audits my installed apps with a local model —
what each app is, whether it earns its place in my workflow, what needs updating,
which licenses and subscriptions I pay for. The distinct thing: analysis is
grounded and local-first — evidence, not guesses, and nothing leaves the Mac by
default.

Built by **Kika** ([aka-kika](https://github.com/aka-kika)). The app I open to
decide what stays on my Mac.

## The milestone

<!-- SF Symbol: flag.checkered -->
1.6.0 is the clarity release. The detail header's ten look-alike cubes — the
part of the app even I kept misreading — became seven that explain themselves:
money things merged into one License cube, an instant label strip replaced the
slow tooltips, and the developer-only tools now hide behind a Developer Mode
toggle. Every license state got its own face and color, mirrored in the sidebar.

## What shipped in 1.6.0

<!-- SF Symbol: shippingbox -->
- **License cube** — license key, subscription, and Paid/Free marks merged into one cube with a single tabbed popover (Key | Subscription). Save closes the panel; the right-click menu shows only what makes sense for the app's current state.
- **License faces and colors** — the cube face carries the license type: Lifetime ∞ (indigo), One-time ① (purple), Annual calendar (cyan), plain key otherwise; App Store seal (blue), subscription card (teal, orange near renewal), Free gift (green).
- **Sidebar license badge** — the same icon and color appear next to the app's name in the list, derived by the exact same logic.
- **Instant label strip** — a caption line under the cubes names the hovered cube and its live state with zero tooltip delay.
- **Developer Mode** — a Settings toggle gates all My App UI (hammer and docs cubes, sidebar hammer badges, My Apps filter). Off by default; marks survive toggling.
- **Self-balancing grid** — one line up to 5 cubes, otherwise two even rows (3+3, 4+3, 4+4, 5+4). No ragged tails.

## The story (how 1.6.0 came together)

<!-- SF Symbol: book.closed -->
- It started with an honest confession: "even me I get confused what is what." Ten cubes, ten tooltips, one second and a half of hover before anything explained itself.
- The fix came in layers across one long day: merge the money cubes, label instantly, gate the developer tools — then keep iterating live against the Sift2 side-build: rename Money to License, close the popover on Save, make the right-click menu state-aware, balance the grid, give every license kind its own face and color, and mirror it all in the sidebar.
- A subagent fleet built the first wave task-by-task with reviews between; the polish wave was hand-tuned screenshot by screenshot.
- The "save didn't work" bug turned out to be a feedback bug — the save always worked; the panel just never said so. Now it closes, and that's the confirmation.
