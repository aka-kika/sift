## What it is

<!-- SF Symbol: app -->
Sift is a native macOS app that audits my installed apps with a local model —
what each app is, whether it earns its place in my workflow, what needs updating,
which licenses and subscriptions I pay for. The distinct thing: analysis is
grounded and local-first — evidence, not guesses, and nothing leaves the Mac by
default.

Built by **Kika** ([aka-kika](https://github.com/aka-kika)). The app I open to
decide what stays on my Mac.

## The milestone

<!-- SF Symbol: flag.checkered -->
1.7.0 closes the loop: Sift always knew what an app is and what it costs — now
it can carry out the verdict. Right-click, Uninstall, and the app plus every
leftover goes to the Trash, while the license record stays in the vault. Shipped
the same day as 1.6.0, in the same sitting.

## What shipped in 1.7.0

<!-- SF Symbol: shippingbox -->
- **Uninstall sweep** — right-click → Uninstall… lists the bundle and every bundle-ID-matched leftover with sizes and match reasons, pre-checked; Move to Trash is recoverable by design. Running apps offer Quit & Continue; Homebrew casks can run brew uninstall; Apple system apps and Sift itself are protected. License keys, notes, and marks survive — the vault lists gone apps under "No longer installed".
- **Symmetric grid** — the Cross-App cube retired (the Similar-apps section kept its own refresh); the cube grid now sits at 3+3 / 4+4 in everyday states.
- **One License entry in the sidebar menu** — the subscription toggle and key-sheet trio collapsed into Add/Edit License…, which opens the detail License popover directly; Copy Key stays.
- **Quieter popover** — the Paid pill is gone (Mark as Paid lives in the cube's right-click for keyless purchases); the Free pill shows only while nothing stronger is recorded.
- **Vault icons fixed** — App Store purchases show their icons (live-icon fallback + capture on Mark as Paid).
