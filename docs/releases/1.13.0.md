Sift 1.13.0 is a polish round from your evening with Sift: better answers for App Store apps, a cleaner window, and a quick search.

**Analysis**
- **App Store apps:** "What is this?" now reads the developer's own App Store description. It no longer says a listing has no description when it has one.

**Window**
- **One straight edge:** the sidebar floats in the macOS 26 style, with no dark divider lines and no gray band.
- **Search:** click the magnifying glass, or press Command-F or Command-K. Type, Return opens the app, Escape closes.
- **Rescan** moved out of the toolbar so a stray click never rescans everything. It is in the File menu, Command-R.
- **Last Added:** a new sort, newest installed apps first.

**Small things**
- **One star color:** favorites are yellow everywhere.
- **No purple ring:** the hammer beside the name already marks the apps you build.
- **Project folder:** its card shows a folder with a plus.
- **Sheets:** Notes, App link and your description all put Clear on the left, Cancel and Save on the right.

Your data and license keys carry over untouched.
