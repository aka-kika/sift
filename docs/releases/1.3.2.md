## What it is

<!-- SF Symbol: app -->
Sift audits everything in /Applications — what each app is, how well it fits your
workflow, update status, and what you own. Analysis runs on local AI, Apple
Intelligence by default. Built by **Veronica Loren**
([aka-kika](https://github.com/aka-kika)).

## The patch

<!-- SF Symbol: flag.checkered -->
A follow-on to 1.3.1's on-device work: a curated **Known-Apps registry** that
hands the model ground truth for apps it already recognizes, so the small
on-device model stops inventing purposes when scanned metadata is thin.

## What shipped in 1.3.2

<!-- SF Symbol: shippingbox -->
- **Known-Apps grounding** — ~20 common apps (VS Code, Raycast, Obsidian, Hazel,
  Figma, Slack, Docker, 1Password, …) carry a curated official link + a one-line
  factual summary, keyed by bundle ID. On a match, that summary is injected into
  the prompt as authoritative **"Verified facts"** for *every* engine, and the
  official link is resolved **offline** (so the link field is prefilled and the
  page-scraper hits the right page). Adding an app is a one-line edit; re-analyze
  to apply. This is the targeted fix for "the analysis started ok, then went
  sideways, inventing" on Apple Silicon Macs without Private Cloud Compute.

## How it was verified

<!-- SF Symbol: checkmark.seal -->
The feature was authored in a toolchain-less environment (never compiled) and
handed off via a branch; it was grafted onto the 1.3.1 line here, built clean,
and confirmed: 81 tests green, seeded bundle IDs verified against a real Mac, and
a live re-analysis of Hazel produced an explanation matching the curated summary
with the official link suggested offline.
